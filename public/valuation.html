Valuation · 方法与口径

先选依据，再选方法

内在估值问的是“未来现金流值多少”，相对定价问的是“可比资产在市场上卖多少”。两者可以交叉验证，但都依赖明确的假设和一致的数据口径。你可以做单场景估值，也可以用保守、乐观情景表达不同判断。

按达摩达兰的基本框架，主要路径是现金流折现、相对定价和或有权益估值。资产基础方法从资产逐项估算；SOTP 是把多种方法组合起来的分部加总框架。下列方法均可在工作台使用，并支持单场景或保守／乐观情景。

先对齐三个口径

口径核对事项
企业 / 股权FCFF 对应 WACC；企业价值转股权价值时扣净负债并核对其他索偿。FCFE、DDM 对应股权成本。P/E、P/B、P/S 直接是股权口径；EV/EBITDA、EV/Sales 是企业口径，避免重复加减债务与现金。
期间 / 币种TTM、年度、预测年度不能混用；金额与股数、报价币种、汇率及稀释口径要一致。当前带预测年数的倍数模型给出预测期价格，未自动折现到今天；与 DCF 现值比较前应统一日期。
盈利 / 现金流经调整 P/E 属于盈利输入口径，不是独立估值流派。一次性与非现金调整需逐项核实；股权激励仍可能造成经济成本与稀释。分部毛利不能直接当净利润乘 PE，EBITDA 也不等于自由现金流。

PEG 的公式与限制

PEG = P/E ÷ 预期 EPS 增速（百分数）
例如：P/E = 20，预期增速 = 10% → PEG = 20 ÷ 10 = 2
模型反推：目标 P/E = 假设 PEG × 预期增速百分数

PEG 不是 PE 乘增长率。增长期限、风险、派息和再投资回报不同，会使 PEG 不可直接比较；1 或 1–2 不是通用合理标准。亏损、低增长或强周期公司尤其不适合机械套用。

模型假设与适用边界

如何选择和交叉验证

先读业务与财报，确认现金流、盈利、净资产或收入哪一个指标有经济意义；再选择对应方法。可比倍数需要解释风险、利润率、资本投入和成长差异，历史分位只是定价背景，不是内在价值或买卖信号。

小米一类多业务公司可以先拆分硬件、互联网服务和汽车等业务，再分别研究方法和假设。选择 SOTP，可将硬件用 PE、汽车用 EV/Sales 等方法分别估值，再按持股比例、汇率和集团调整汇总。原 PE“分业务”仍适用于共用一个目标 PE 的情形。

用在估值钟摆中

把确认后的单点或区间设为钟摆依据，另外设置安全边际与泡沫阈值。击球区 ≤ 低点 ×（1−安全边际）；泡沫区 > 高点 ×（1+泡沫阈值）。这些标签反映你设定的假设，不是确定的价格底部或交易指令。

保存每项数据的报告期与来源，区分已披露事实和未来假设。交叉验证的重点是解释差异，而不是取几种模型的平均数。

Valuation · Methods and definitions

Choose the basis first

Intrinsic valuation asks what future cash flows are worth; relative pricing asks what comparable assets trade for. Both depend on explicit assumptions and consistent data. Use a single case or conservative and optimistic cases to express your views.

Damodaran's main approaches are discounted cash flows, relative valuation and contingent claims. Asset-based methods assess assets individually; SOTP combines methods across businesses. The following calculators support a single case or conservative and optimistic cases.

Align three definitions

DefinitionWhat to check
Enterprise / equityFCFF uses WACC; bridge enterprise value to equity by subtracting net debt and checking other claims. FCFE and DDM use cost of equity. P/E, P/B and P/S use equity value; EV/EBITDA and EV/Sales use enterprise value. Do not count cash or debt twice.
Period / currencyAlign TTM, annual and forecast periods, currency, exchange rates, shares and dilution. Multiples models with a forecast horizon currently return a future price without discounting it to today. Align valuation dates before comparing with DCF present values.
Earnings / cash flowAdjusted P/E is an earnings definition, not a separate valuation school. Reconcile adjustments; non-cash stock compensation may still impose economic costs and dilution. Segment gross profit is not net income for a P/E, and EBITDA is not free cash flow.

PEG: formula and limits

PEG = P/E ÷ expected EPS growth in percentage points
P/E 20 and expected growth 10% → PEG = 20 ÷ 10 = 2
Implied target P/E = assumed PEG × expected growth in percentage points

PEG divides, rather than multiplies, P/E by growth. Growth horizons, risk, payouts and returns on reinvestment affect comparability. Neither 1 nor 1–2 is a universal fair range, particularly for losses, low growth or cyclical earnings.

Model assumptions and boundaries

Choose and cross-check

Read the business disclosures first, then decide whether cash flow, earnings, book equity or revenue is economically meaningful. Explain differences in risk, margins, reinvestment and growth before using peer multiples. Historical percentiles are pricing context, not intrinsic value or trading signals.

For a diversified company such as Xiaomi, assess hardware, internet services and vehicles separately. Use SOTP to combine hardware P/E with vehicle EV/Sales and reconcile ownership, FX and group adjustments. The P/E segment mode remains available for businesses sharing one target P/E.

Use the result on the pendulum

Choose a confirmed point or range, then set the margin of safety and bubble threshold. Strike zone ≤ low × (1−margin); bubble zone > high × (1+threshold). The labels reflect your assumptions, not a guaranteed floor or a trading instruction.

Record each input's period and source, and separate reported facts from future assumptions. Cross-checks should explain differences rather than mechanically average models.
